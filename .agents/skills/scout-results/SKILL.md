# Scout Results

Write results for a scouting director who needs a clear answer about what the data shows and whether it could change a decision.

## Rules

- Lead with the answer in one sentence a scout would say aloud.
- State every effect in the data's own units, with the two group values.
- Give the p-value once, after the effect, in words.
- Give the effect size as a share of a standard deviation and say whether it is large enough to change a decision.
- Round every number to two decimal places, write a p-value below .001 as "less than .001", and never use scientific notation.
- Never use "causes," "drives," or "leads to."
- End every result with one limitation the data cannot rule out.
- No journal formatting and no tables unless asked.
